# Laptop Hardware Requirement

Quantity: **2 laptops** for software developers. Please quote new units and, separately,
certified refurbished units if you stock them (see conditions at the end). Software to be
installed is in `SOFTWARE_LIST_curated.md`.

We already work on this configuration daily, so matching it is sufficient. Please do not
quote above the minimum unless the item is marked "preferred".

## Required (minimum)
- **Processor:** 8 cores / 16 threads, AMD Ryzen 7 (7730U, 7735HS or newer) or Intel Core i7 12th gen or newer
- **Memory:** 16 GB DDR4 or DDR5, **must have two SO-DIMM slots** (not soldered) so it can be upgraded to 32 GB later
- **Storage:** 512 GB NVMe SSD
- **Graphics:** Integrated (AMD Radeon or Intel Iris Xe). No dedicated GPU needed.
- **Display:** 15.6 or 16 inch, 1920×1080 or 1920×1200, anti-glare (non-touch is fine)
- **Operating system:** Windows 11 **Pro**, 64-bit, licensed (not Home)
- **Security:** TPM 2.0, fingerprint reader
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5, 2× USB-C (one with power delivery and DisplayPort), 2× USB-A, HDMI
- **Webcam and microphone:** 1080p webcam with privacy shutter, dual mics
- **Keyboard:** Backlit, English (India/US) layout
- **Battery:** 8 hours real-world use, 45 Wh or larger
- **Weight:** 1.8 kg or less
- **Warranty:** 1 year onsite manufacturer warranty (new) or 1 year seller warranty (refurbished)

## Preferred (quote only if the price difference is small)
- 16 GB installed as 2× 8 GB (dual channel)
- 512 GB or more
- Ethernet (RJ45) port

## Reference models that meet this spec
- Lenovo ThinkBook 16 G7 ARP (Ryzen 7 7735HS, 16 GB, 512 GB, Windows 11 Pro)
- Lenovo ThinkPad E16 Gen 2 AMD (Ryzen 7 7735HS/7735U, 16 GB, 512 GB, Windows 11 Pro)
- Lenovo V15 G4 AMD, Dell 15 (DC15255), HP 15 or ProBook 450 with Ryzen 7 7730U, 16 GB, 512 GB
  (add a Windows 11 Pro licence if the unit ships with Home)

## Refurbished units are acceptable if
- Business line only: Dell Latitude 5520/5530/5540, HP EliteBook 850 G8 / ProBook 450 G8+, Lenovo ThinkPad T15/L15 Gen 2 or newer
- Intel Core i7 11th gen or newer, or Ryzen 7 5000 series PRO or newer
- Grade A cosmetic condition, battery health 80 percent or better (state it on the invoice)
- Windows 11 Pro activated with a genuine licence
- **Minimum 1 year warranty stated on the invoice**

## Please include in the quote
- Exact model number and SKU, processor, RAM configuration (slots used), SSD, OS edition
- Unit price, GST, delivery charge, delivery time
- Warranty type (onsite/carry-in), duration, and who provides it
