# Evaluation of Laptop Quotation GIB/247/2026-27

Quotation from Genius Infoway Bangalore dated 23/09/2026, evaluated on 2026-09-23 against
`HARDWARE_REQUIREMENT.md` (what we asked for) and current Indian retail prices. Prices are in
INR and **GST-inclusive** unless marked; IndiaMART sellers list ex-GST, so those figures are
shown as listed × 1.18. The **Verified** column says whether the price was read from the
retailer page ("Page read"), came from a search snippet, or was not found. Re-check on the day
of purchase.

## 1. Headline

- **Nothing in the quote answers the spec we sent.** We asked for 2 developer laptops: 16 GB
  with two SO-DIMM slots, 512 GB, integrated GPU, 15.6/16 inch, 1.8 kg or less, Windows 11 Pro,
  1-year onsite, and "do not quote above the minimum". Reference band in
  `HARDWARE_RECOMMENDATION.md`: ThinkBook 16 G7 ARP at ₹72,675 with Pro. Every new line in the
  quote is a workstation at 2 to 3 times that; the cheapest new unit is ₹1,45,140.
- **Both refurbished lines fail hard requirements**: 30-day warranty against our 1-year minimum
  on the invoice, and the Latitude 5420 is a 14 inch machine.
- **Dell Pro Max 16 fails the weight limit**: Dell's page lists a minimum of 2.11 kg.
- **Terms contradict the line items**: page 2 says "Direct Carry in Warranty" while the line items
  say "3 Years Onsite". Also 100% payment in advance, quotation valid for 1 day, no return or
  cancellation once the PO is issued, and delivery charged extra without a figure.
- **Missing from every line**: exact SKU / model number and RAM slot configuration (only the Dell
  line says 8 GB × 2). This matters: the ZBook 8 G1i 16 alone exists in touch / non-touch and
  Arc / RTX variants spanning ₹1.75 lakh to ₹2.31 lakh, so the quoted line cannot be matched to
  a retail price with certainty.

## 3. Recommendation

1. Do not accept any line as quoted. Send `HARDWARE_REQUIREMENT.md` again and ask for a quote on
   the reference models: Lenovo ThinkBook 16 G7 ARP or ThinkPad E16 Gen 2 AMD, 16 GB in two
   slots, 512 GB, Windows 11 Pro, 1-year onsite. Expected price ₹65k to ₹75k per unit.
2. If a workstation-class machine is wanted anyway, the only defensible picks are the
   **ThinkPad E16 Gen 3** (item 4) at or below ₹1,35,000, or the **ThinkPad P16s Gen 4** (item 5)
   at the reseller price of about ₹1,86,000 with the 3-year Premier + ADP warranty matched.
3. Reject both refurbished lines unless re-quoted with a 1-year warranty on the invoice at about
   ₹40,000 (Latitude 5420) and ₹59,000 (Precision 5560).
4. Do not pay 100% in advance on a 1-day-validity quote with no cancellation. Ask for payment on
   delivery or at most 50% advance, and 30-day validity.

## 4. Questions to send the vendor

- Exact SKU / part number for each line (for example C1JP4PT vs C1KK6PT for the ZBook).
- RAM configuration: how many SO-DIMM slots, how many populated, maximum supported.
- Weight of each unit.
- Delivery charge as a figure, and delivery time.
- Warranty in writing per line: onsite or carry-in, duration, who provides it (line items say
  onsite, terms page says carry-in).
- For refurbished units: cosmetic grade, battery health percentage, and a minimum 1-year warranty
  stated on the invoice.
- Revised payment terms and validity as in section 3.
